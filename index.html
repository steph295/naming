Change of name

A change of name is a one-off application for a horse that meets the eligibility criteria below. A £60 fee (inc. VAT) applies on submission. Approval is at Weatherbys' discretion.

Eligibility criteria

  • The horse has not yet started in a race.Once a horse has raced under its name — flat, jumps, or point-to-point — the name becomes part of its record and cannot be changed.
  • The horse has a currently registered name.Unnamed horses and reservations that haven't been registered to a horse don't qualify — register the name first, or pick a new one.
  • You are the registered owner.For shared ownership, written consent from every co-owner must be obtained before the new name is approved.
The new name must pass the same checks as a new registration: 18 characters max, no commercial or offensive content, and no clash with famous persons or existing names.
